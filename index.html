This is how the EU de-risks its €10bn Gigafactories: the public side acts as anchor customer, pre-purchasing compute access worth up to 17% of IT CAPEX (matched by the host state), paid from operational readiness over 5 years, with expansion funds released only after obligations are met and a technical audit passes. Applied to GAF: Oyu Tolgoi (and optionally a second anchor) signs a 5-year offtake at L2/L3 rates. Toggle it in the controls — the floor lifts the blended curve's Year 1 and makes the weak DSCR years (2026–28 in the 10-yr model) lender-visible. The offtake is not extra revenue; it is the services revenue made contractual.

Relation to the 10-year financial model. A separate project-finance model exists (GreenAIFactory_10yr_FinancialModel_vFINAL, Mar 2026): 80/20 debt-equity, four debt tranches, DSRA, CIT holiday, expansions to 384 GPUs. Its headline returns — equity IRR 32.1%, project IRR 23.8% — were independently re-verified (Aug 2026) and are driven by the blended services mix with escalating service rates ($7.50 → $22.50/GPU-hr), not by rental. This L6 layer deliberately remains the unlevered floor case: pure GPU rental at $4.50/hr, no debt, no services escalation — the two views bracket the business. Full audit 18 Aug 2026 (VAL-0001 §E.1): all seven sheets re-derived independently — every year reproduces to the cent; equity IRR 32.07%, project IRR 23.81% pre-tax (≈21.5% after tax), NPV cell $7,329,245 = equity NPV (FCFF $9.03M), MOIC 9.19× cash-on-cash (quote 9–10×, not 15×). Watch-items: the AI-services rate multiplies $7.50 by customer count on the same GPU pool — at a flat $7.50 the 10-yr cumulative FCFE flips +$25.1M → −$7.4M, so contracted offtakes are the case; DSCR breaches the 1.25× covenant in 2026/27/28 (1.02/0.53/1.13, DSRA covers only the 2027 cash gap); book equity negative 2027–28; 2028 ending cash $6.6k; Y1 power $143k vs engineering $77.4k (fix lifts equity IRR to ~35%).
