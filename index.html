1 Project goals

The Green AI Factory GAF-64 project shall deploy an integrated, production-operable AI infrastructure centered on 64 high-memory accelerators. The platform is intended for model development and training, inference and AI services, applied research, public-sector and industrial workloads, and skills/ecosystem activities. The procurement object is the Supplier-delivered IT system and its verified integration at the defined facility interfaces.

The technical structure follows the EuroHPC INNOVATE Part 2 specification pattern, while compute scale and service content are adapted to the GAF-64 project. EuroHPC AI Factory requirements are incorporated only where technically applicable. Hosting-entity obligations—staffing, ecosystem governance, user allocation, partnerships and reporting—remain Owner obligations and are not misassigned to the equipment Supplier.
